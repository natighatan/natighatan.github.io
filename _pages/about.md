Hi, I'm Nati.

I'm an applied research scientist, working on machine learning, data science, and a wide variety of data-related challenges. My academic background is in computational neuroscience, a field that brought together two subjects I've always found fascinating: the human brain and computation.

My curiosity extends far beyond my professional interests, spanning everything from science and mathematics to psychology, etymology, music, and art. I enjoy beauty in all its forms. Learning how things work makes me happy. So does exploring how ideas develop and discovering the curious stories behind things we tend to take for granted.

This website is a place to share that curiosity with others. It's a personal collection of things I find interesting enough to explore and think about. There's no particular theme or agenda, and no promise of regular updates. Just whatever happens to capture my curiosity.

I hope you'll find something here that interests you, too.
